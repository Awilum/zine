# ZINE Demo Collection

All demos are self-contained and use fictional English editorial content inspired by Vietnam. The images are generated abstract gradients and patterns in 16:9 or 9:16 formats; they contain no logos, recognizable people, or third-party artwork.

## Complete demo

`complete-demo` exercises the engine's layout vocabulary.

## Travel book

`travel-book` demonstrates chapters, chapter openers, contents, frontmatter, backmatter, and pagination.

## Zine

`zine` demonstrates a compact image-led publication.

## Travel guide

`travel-guide` demonstrates practical editorial structure and mixed layouts.

## Travel journal

`travel-journal` demonstrates diary-style entries and visual notes.

## Photo essay

`photo-essay` demonstrates a narrative essay built from photographs, text, and quotations.
